How to play

🦟 A mosquito flies in, then a dashed line shows where it will dive. Tap it before it reaches the sleeper.

🔥 Chain kills for a combo and a score multiplier. Tapping empty space breaks it.

🧴 Spray kills everything. ⏱️ Slow-mo slows time. 🟡 Golden ones are worth 5 but fly away.

👑 A Queen arrives every 25 points and takes 5 hits.

🪙 Earn coins, finish daily missions and unlock blankets.
